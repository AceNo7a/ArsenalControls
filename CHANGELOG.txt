1.0.0-beta.2 - 2026-11-10

- Remove the Arsenal-only startup restriction.
- Open the UI immediately, even when Arsenal modules are absent.
- Connect optional weapon adapters asynchronously, only in Arsenal.
- Use ordinary Humanoid health in other games and explain unavailable weapons.
- Preserve the existing public loader URL.

1.0.0-beta.1 - 2026-11-10

First packaged public beta, based on optimized development build V6.
- Readable formatted source, deterministic offline build and pinned SlimeUI.
- Versioned UI, About tab, credits, reset settings and unload controls.
- F8 stops every active feature, including ESP, and clears its visuals.
- Unsupported-game check and bounded startup wait for expected client modules.
- Feature-readiness checks and duplicate runtime-error notification suppression.
- Independent cleanup of movement overrides, hooks, events and render binding.
- Internal test API and performance counters removed from the release.
- Reduced decorative motion and cached target, visibility and character lookups.
- Silent aim and client ammo refill explicitly marked experimental.

Retained: aim, FOV/smoothing, target lock, team/wall filters, auto fire,
autoplay, ESP, local recoil/ammo options, flight, noclip and speed override.
No kill-all, invulnerability, server vulnerability claim or damage guarantee.

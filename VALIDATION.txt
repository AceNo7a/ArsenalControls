INHERITED V6 CHECKS (BEFORE RELEASE CLEANUP)
Camera tracking observed with wall check temporarily disabled for measurement.
Local speed override restored the captured value; noclip restored cached
collisions; flight constraints were created and removed. Silent cast override
installed only when enabled and was removed when disabled. Local recoil
restoration was checked. These are local behavior checks, not server promises.

LIMITS
The release smoke checks ran in one connected Madium client. The character
was dead for portions of the checks; no new full combat/autoplay match was
completed for this package. Silent-aim damage and unlimited server-accepted
ammo are unverified. Other executors, all maps, long sessions and future
Arsenal updates have not been covered. No universal FPS improvement is claimed.
The unsupported-universe guard was inspected but not live-tested in another
game. Public-beta labeling reflects these limits.

Packaging does not publish this release to a website or repository.
